## Plain script (for the teleprompter)

Rare disease families often become researchers themselves, searching disconnected databases and research networks for anyone who has studied something similar.

RarePath Atlas finds those connections, with evidence behind each one. Take CDKL5 deficiency disorder. It was once labelled a Rett syndrome variant. Today it's recognised as its own disorder.

Yet people with CDD were enrolled in the same NIH natural-history study as Rett. That's real, shared research infrastructure.

And that shared work informed a severity scale built specifically for CDD.

But shared infrastructure isn't shared biology. Nothing here says Rett treatments carry over.

OpenAI pulls claims from the sources, each tied to an exact quote, then explains them plainly and points to a concrete next question for researchers.

Rare shouldn't mean researching alone.
